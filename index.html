Chasing the Emerald
The Hidden Rhythms of Seattle Air Travel

Seattle draws millions of visitors each year, but how do most people get there, and when is the best time to go? This interactive article explores travel patterns to the Emerald City, beginning with a comparison of different transportation modes. We’ll uncover why flights dominate as the most popular way to reach Seattle, highlight the top airlines chosen by travelers, and reveal the best times of year to plan your trip based on real travel data.

How We Traveled Then vs. Now
In the 1800s, traveling from New York City to Seattle meant weeks at sea. People boarded ships that sailed down the Atlantic, around Cape Horn at the tip of South America, and finally up the Pacific Coast. By the early 1900s, the completion of the railroads transformed the journey. What once took months by boat now took about a week by train, shifting long-distance travel from ocean routes to cross-country rail networks.
From Boats to Planes
Today, in the 2000s, the dominant travel mode is air. A trip that once demanded months of hardship or days of rail travel can now be completed in a few hours by plane.
Where Seattle’s Travelers Come From?
And as air travel became the primary mode of transportation, Seattle grew into a major hub in the Pacific Northwest. This was shaped not only by who traveled from the city, but also by who traveled to it. Today, the most popular origins for flights into Seattle reflect a mix of regional ties, economic connections, and long-standing mobility patterns.
